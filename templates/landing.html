Transfer Algorithm Haversine Distance-Aware
1. Depletion Velocity Forecasting

Instead of looking only at static counts, our engine calculates Days Remaining using average daily consumption rate against replenishment lead times. Clinics are alerted before they run out.

days_remaining = usable_stock / avg_daily_usage
2. Distance-Aware Surplus Matching

When a shortage is identified, the system calculates available surplus in nearby health centres while strictly protecting the supplying clinic's safety stock, ranking by distance and spare capacity.

safe_surplus = max(0, usable - safety_stock)
3. Public Availability Transparency

Patients and caregivers can verify reported stock levels, facility phone numbers, and last verified updates online before travelling kilometres with sick relatives.

Verified status • Last updated timestamp • Direct helpline
